# CLOUD READING
### Creator: Ben

#### Concept and Implementation:
In this project, you look at the clouds floating through the sky and write down what you think it looks like, and your answers pop on the screen, including answers from others as well. 

#### What worked:
What I really loved about this project was the nostalgic look of the Windows XP user interface, I thought it was implemented really well, and the face that you can see all previous guesses and that there is a variety of cloud shapes to look at, as well as having your own answer pop up alongside the others instead of deleting them. 

#### What could be improved + Suggestion:
It would be really nice to see a few more cloud shapes! I understand it was a mini project, but maybe in the future, there could be more cloud formations for users to choose from. 

Overall, this was a really well-rounded project, and it was super fun to see! 


# FACE DE BOOK
### Creator: Kerven

#### Concept and Implementation:
Kerven's project is supposed to be a parody of Facebook, where users log in and find out they've actually been scammed and need to pay ransom in order to get their account back.

#### What worked:
I love the concept of this project, it gave me a good laugh, and I liked how simple it looked. I definitely gave off those vibes of joke websites that are really just for fun, which I loved about it. 

#### What could be improved + Suggestion:
I think that making the interface actually resemble Facebook would make the project even funnier and strengthen the overall concept. While I liked the simplicity of the design, I feel that adding a few more details and visual elements from the original platform would make the parody more recognizable and entertaining. I think for the future, Kerven could add more features from Facebook itself, to allow users a little more freedom, and then have the ransom popup appear randomly! That would be really fun! 

# Bubbles Against The World
### Creator: Robert

#### Concept and Implementation:
Robert's Project is a bubble popping game where you have to pop as many bubbles as you can within the time limit, and at the end, the amount of bubbles you popped and where your mouse moved is tracked and shown to you. 

#### What worked:
I really like the concept of this project. Having user mouse data tracked was really cool considering the lightheartedness of the concept. Using the Pokemon GO analogy in his explanation really solidified the idea. 

#### What could be improved + Suggestion:
I think if he added more user tracking data, that could make it seem scarier as a concept. Alongside mouse movement, it could track the time in ms it took to pop the bubbles and like Nat's project, their hesitation. That would be a really interesting aspect!
